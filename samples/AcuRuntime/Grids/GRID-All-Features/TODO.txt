AcuRuntime:
when adjustable-pressed and moveable-pressed
When you click on either the "ADJUSTABLE-COLUMNS" or the "MOVEABLE-COLUMNS",
a "modify grid-1" is executed.
This makes the down-ward arrow of the grid scroll-bar to disappear.

AcuRuntime:
At the same time, the horizontal scroll-bar should appear as soon as you enlarge a column enough.
Unfortunately, this does not occur easily and always.
(if you take the standard gridctl.cbl the horizontal scroll-bar is shown every time the grid goes beyond the right-hand side border)
I tried to add HSCROLL directly in the screen section, but I see it gives no improvements.
Just compile with the following command-line to include:
ccbl32 -ga -si HSCROLL gridctl-MoveAndHide.cbl
or exclude (default) the horizontal bar:
ccbl32 -ga -sx HSCROLL gridctl-MoveAndHide.cbl

AcuRuntime:
You can hide the first column, even if it's designed as a row header.
I can avoid this in the code, by starting loops from 2 (ws-first-usable-col), but it should be automatic.
Unless I've missed anything in my code.
Just compile with the following command-line to include (default):
ccbl32 -ga -si ROW gridctl-MoveAndHide.cbl
or exclude the Row Header from the count and make it hideable:
ccbl32 -ga -sx ROW gridctl-MoveAndHide.cbl
